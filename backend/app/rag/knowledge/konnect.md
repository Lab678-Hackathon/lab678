# KONNECT — Service FAQ Knowledge Base
Version: v2 presentation reference (2026-10-21)
Language: English
Purpose: Answer questions about using KONNECT itself, not general Seoul tourism.
Scope note: Exact login requirements and usage limits are intentionally omitted because they may change before the presentation.

## What is KONNECT?
KONNECT helps independent travelers in Seoul find cultural experiences that fit their available time, starting location, and interests. It focuses on hands-on cultural activities, performances, exhibitions, cultural and historic places, and festivals or cultural events.

## What are the two main features?
Find something cultural to do now helps you choose an individual experience for your available time. Create a cultural route helps you plan multiple cultural experiences for a selected date. KONNECT focuses on cultural activities, not a complete multi-day travel itinerary.

## How do I find an experience?
Enter your starting point, start time, and Available until time. You can also describe interests or preferences in the AI conversation. You may enter your starting point manually instead of sharing your location. If you have another appointment afterward, leave enough travel time when setting Available until; KONNECT does not automatically calculate that onward journey.

## How do I create a cultural route?
Provide the date, starting point, and available start and end times through the guided conversation. You can add interests or other preferences. KONNECT aims to connect at least two suitable cultural experiences in a feasible order, without adding unnecessary stops just to fill your available time. The number of stops depends on your conditions.

## What do the recommendation labels mean?
Fits your conditions: Available information supports a match with your relevant conditions. This does not guarantee admission.
Partially confirmed: No relevant conflict is confirmed, but important details, such as a price or reservation requirement, are still unverified.
Alternative: The option differs from one or more of your requested conditions; the difference should be explained.

## Can I book tickets or pay through KONNECT, and are details guaranteed?
KONNECT does not handle ticket reservations or payments. When available, it provides links to official information or reservation pages. Prices, opening hours, reservation rules, and admission may change. Missing information does not mean an experience is free, requires no reservation, or is guaranteed to be available.

## What are My Pick and My Route?
My Pick is where you can return to your currently saved experience from the immediate recommendation flow. My Route is where you can return to your currently saved cultural route. These features do not imply that KONNECT stores your complete travel or visit history.

## What can I ask the AI assistant?
You can ask questions about KONNECT and its features within the existing AI conversations in the experience and route flows. KONNECT's FAQ assistance focuses on using this cultural-experience service. Ordinary restaurant recommendations, shopping suggestions, and unrelated travel questions are outside this FAQ's scope. For those requests, the assistant should briefly explain KONNECT's focus and guide you toward a relevant cultural-experience question.
